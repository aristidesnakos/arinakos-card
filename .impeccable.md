## Design Context

### Users
- **Scanners**: people Ari just met who scanned the printed QR or his phone. They're standing, on a phone, often mid-conversation. Their job: save Ari's contact in one tap. Everything on `/` serves that first.
- **Curious visitors**: people who come back later (from the saved contact, LinkedIn or a link) to see who Ari is. Their job: understand what he does (agency, paid call) and what he has built on his own (`/work`).

### Brand Personality
Sharp, credible, curious, crafted. A senior technical operator who also builds things out of genuine interest.
The agency (The Auspicious Company, fractional CTO for regulated industries) and the paid call sell; the portfolio
does not. It shows separate personal ventures honestly, each for what it is, with no forced common theme and no
claims or metrics beyond what each product's own site says.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Refined editorial: like a well-made printed card.
- Palette "Stone and Cobalt" (Ari's pick, 18 Nov 2026, replacing navy and gold on the site): light = stone #F4F4F2, graphite text #18181A,
  secondary #55555C, cobalt #2340B0 for the one button and small details; dark = #111214, text #ECECEE, secondary #A4A5AD,
  periwinkle #9DB0FF for button and details. Cobalt is rare: button, photo ring, arrows, section rules. Navy and gold remain only in the
  printed QR art, the Wallet pass and the /me home-screen icons.
- One self-hosted display serif for the name and headings, system sans for body (performance budget allows one font file).
- Lists with hairline rules over boxed buttons; left-aligned, asymmetric; whitespace does the work.
- Anti-references: SaaS landing pages, card grids of identical tiles, gradients, glassmorphism, link-in-bio templates.
- Hard constraints: static, no third-party anything (scripts, fonts, embeds, tracking, cookies), hash-only CSP per page,
  18px minimum text, 48px targets, WCAG AA in light and dark, Lighthouse mobile Perf ≥ 95 and A11y 100.

### Design Principles
1. Save contact is always the obvious first action on `/` on a phone.
2. Say only what's true and verifiable; the product's own site is the source for its one-liner.
3. Separate things look separate: clusters are distinct sections, not one uniform grid.
4. Restraint over decoration: the cobalt accent is rare, motion is minimal and respects reduced motion.
5. Every page stays light enough to load instantly on a phone at a meetup.
